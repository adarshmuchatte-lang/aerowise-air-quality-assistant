# AeroWise 3.0 — hackathon pitch

## One sentence

**AeroWise turns the air forecast above you into a clear plan for your household and your day.**

## The problem

Families often see an AQI score and still have to figure out what to do next: whether to shift an outdoor plan, what habit might help at home, or if a daily trip has a practical alternative. A number alone does not answer those questions.

## The experience

AeroWise pairs a location's modeled air-quality forecast with an interactive 3D Earth, hourly trend, and a few suggestions shaped by household and routine preferences. A visual indoor-air room and a simple trip calculator make everyday choices easier to explore. The AI assistant is optional; built-in guidance keeps the demo useful without a key.

The planet and atmospheric effects are visual explanations, not measured pollution plumes. Each reading is clearly labeled as live modeled data, stale modeled data, or demo data.

## 45-second demo

1. Search for **London** or another city. Show the live modeled AQI, pollutant values, and city marker on the 3D Earth.
2. Open the hourly forecast and select a point to compare the next few hours.
3. Choose a household and commute routine. Show how the daily suggestions respond to the location and preferences.
4. Try a room control, then compare a car trip with another travel option in the CO₂e calculator.
5. Save or share the plan, then ask the assistant what to change about today's commute.

If live data cannot load during judging, select **Demo View**. Its fixed example values are labeled clearly and never presented as real measurements.

## Why it is useful

- It translates an air-quality forecast into decisions people already make.
- It connects timing, home habits, and travel in one place.
- It adapts suggestions to a household and routine without claiming precise personal exposure reductions.
- It supports city search, a user-controlled location request, share, and a locally saved daily plan.
- It remains demoable without a provider key or an AI key.

## Product boundaries

Open-Meteo forecast values are modeled estimates, not nearby regulatory monitor readings. The room is a conceptual habit visualizer, and the trip calculator estimates direct-use CO₂e from editable distance assumptions. AeroWise is a planning aid, not an official alert service or medical tool. Follow local public-health guidance for health decisions.
